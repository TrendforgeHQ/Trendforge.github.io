## New Design and Release Plan
Fairphone has announced that the Fairbuds 2 will debut on October 14, beginning sales in Europe at €119 before a U.S. launch at $119. The company revealed the plan after leaks of a major redesign appeared earlier in the month and an official teaser was posted to X yesterday.

## Modular Architecture Returns
The earbuds keep the repair‑able modular concept of the original Fairbuds. Seven fully replaceable parts will be available, a detail that builds on the first model’s design that already featured a modular approach. This continuity reinforces Fairphone’s focus on sustainability.

## Codec Upgrade
The Fairbuds 2 adds support for Sony’s high‑quality adaptive LDAC Bluetooth codec. The initial Fairbuds model could use only SBC and AAC codecs, so the new version offers a noticeable improvement in audio quality.

## Pricing Context
At launch, the original Fairbuds sold for €149, roughly $162 at the time. The new earbuds are priced lower, at €119 in Europe and $119 in the U.S., making them more affordable while maintaining the modular ethos.

## What Readers Should Watch
Consumers interested in repair‑friendly audio gear should note the October 14 release date and the lower price point. The addition of LDAC suggests a better listening experience, especially for users of Sony devices. As the earbuds become available, tracking availability and any early review coverage will help gauge real‑world performance and repair feasibility.
