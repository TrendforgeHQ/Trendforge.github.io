## Prime Big Deal Days Starts Before October
Amazon’s annual Prime Big Deal Days sale begins at 12:01 am PT on October 6 and runs through 11:59 pm PT on October 7, which ends at 2:59 am ET on October 8.The event begins a few days before the official kickoff of October Prime Day.

## Early Deals on Amazon Hardware
Amazon is already discounting its own devices. From Ring to Blink and Echo devices to Fire tablets, early buyers can save up to 77 % on a range of smart‑home hardware. The largest Echo Show, the 21‑inch model, combines a smart display and Fire TV; its 21‑inch screen is large enough to show calendar, shopping list, weather, and other widgets in one view. The Echo Spot also comes with Alexa+.

## How to Access the Sale
You may qualify for six months free if you’re aged 18‑24. These options allow non‑members to enjoy the same early‑month discounts that Prime members receive.

## Popular Early‑October Deals
 In the kitchen, Ninja, Breville, and De’Longhi offer some of the biggest early‑month discounts. Robotic vacuum brands such as iRobot and Shark see significant markdowns, with iRobot’s Roombas at 50‑57 % off and many Shark models below their 2026 lows. The Ring Battery Doorbell 2K and the iRobot Roomba 105X Combo are highlighted as top picks.

## Headphone and Audio Savings
 Bose QuietComfort 2nd‑gen headphones are listed at $319 with a $40 saving, while Panasonic Technics EAH‑AZ100 wireless earbuds are at $247.99, saving $52. Sony WH‑CH720N headphones are available for $98, a discount of $81.99. These offers appeared ahead of the main sale, helping shoppers avoid the chaos of the peak‑hour shopping.

## Timing and Updates
The page with early deals will be updated each morning through the end of the sale, ensuring that shoppers see the latest markdowns. Deals on Ring, Blink, and Fire tablets began on September 23, with Shark, Bissell, Ninja, and Sonicare following on September 24, and Echo and eero joining on September 27.

## What to Watch As the Sale Continues
Because the Prime Big Deal Days event is a preview, many deals that appear early will likely recur during Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Early shoppers should keep an eye on price changes, as some items have fluctuated in the lead‑up, such as the AirPods Pro dropping to $199 before rising again.

## Bottom Line
Amazon’s early‑October Prime Big Deal Days deals are already live, offering up to 77 % off on smart‑home and other popular gadgets. Non‑Prime members can join a free trial and may qualify for a 6‑month free period if they are aged 18‑24.
