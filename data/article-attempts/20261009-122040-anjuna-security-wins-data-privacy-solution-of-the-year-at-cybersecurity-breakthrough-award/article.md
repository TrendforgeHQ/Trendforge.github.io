## Award Announcement
Anjuna Security was named the 2026 winner of the Data Privacy Solution of the Year award in the 10th annual CyberSecurity Breakthrough Awards program. The award was presented by CyberSecurity Breakthrough, an independent market intelligence organization that has been recognizing breakthrough cybersecurity innovation for a decade.

## Context of the Awards
The 10th program received thousands of nominations from organizations in more than 20 countries. Winners were chosen for innovation, technological advancement, and impact on the industry. The program now spans categories such as Cloud Security, Threat Intelligence, Risk Management, Fraud Prevention, Mobile Security, Application Security, and Identity Management.

## Why Anjuna’s Win Matters
Anjuna’s victory underscores a shift from perimeter defenses to privacy‑centric solutions. The company’s approach uses remote attestation and data‑in‑use isolation to reduce risk and guarantee high privacy and security for collaborations. According to Anjuna, this win validates its strategy of freeing organizations from data‑sharing constraints.

## Industry Perspective
Steve Johansson, Managing Director of CyberSecurity Breakthrough, noted that AI models introduce new risk vectors that traditional tools, like legacy data clean rooms, cannot protect against. He said the program’s winners embody cutting‑edge cybersecurity technology, delivering next‑generation protection in today’s complex threat landscape.

## Takeaway for Readers
The award signals a broader industry trend toward confidential computing and privacy‑first architectures. Businesses seeking to protect data in collaborative environments should examine solutions that offer remote attestation and isolation, as these capabilities are now recognized as leading the market. While the award does not endorse specific products, it highlights technologies that address emerging AI‑driven risk vectors and the need for scalable, privacy‑focused security.

## What the Evidence Shows
Anjuna Security’s Data Privacy Solution of the Year award, announced by CyberSecurity Breakthrough, reflects a decade‑long expansion of cybersecurity categories and an industry pivot toward privacy‑centric, next‑generation protection techniques amid evolving threat landscapes.
