## Arrest of a Key Executive
The United States has arrested Greg Lui, the chief executive of Earthmade Computer, on three counts related to smuggling. The DOJ accused him of using false documentation to hide the shipment of servers worth more than $300 million that were intended for China. The case follows earlier arrests that have highlighted a broader smuggling network involving Nvidia chips.

## Context of Nvidia’s Compliance Issues
The arrest comes after a senior Nvidia manager in Taiwan was linked to a similar smuggling operation run by former Supermicro staff. Nvidia’s spokesperson told Bloomberg that less than half a percent of its products have allegedly been diverted to China. ## Legal and Regulatory Framework
Lui is charged with violating the Export Control Reform Act, the Export Administration Regulations, federal smuggling statutes, and money‑laundering laws. The case underscores how the U.S. government is treating the export of high‑performance computing hardware as a national‑security matter.
