## Forecast vs. Reality
On June 24, Markus Thielen of 10x Research predicted a Bitcoin trough around $55,000 in late summer and early autumn 2026. Ten days later, on July 4, he tightened that range to $46,000–$47,000 and expected a 30 % rebound to $60,000–$65,000 by year‑end.Bitcoin (CRYPTO:BTC) never dipped to the levels forecasted by Markus Thielen. Bitcoin’s 2026 low was $57,717 on June 30, and the price later climbed 49 % to about $86,000—well above the bear target.

## Why the Gap Matters
With Bitcoin hovering near $86,000, reaching the $46,000–$47,000 level would have required a 24 %‑30 % drop by December 31. This scenario is far from the initial forecast and illustrates how quickly price dynamics can shift.

## Methodological Additions
Thielen also expanded his analysis in July by applying Elliott Wave theory, a method that interprets price charts as repeating wave patterns. The addition of this framework did not alter the end‑of‑year target, but it did signal a more detailed approach to chart interpretation.

## Takeaway for Traders
The divergence between forecast and outcome highlights the risk of relying solely on analyst predictions.
