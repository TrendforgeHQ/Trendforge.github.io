## Revised Forecasts and Market Context

Tom Lee, chairman of BitMine Immersion Technologies (BMNR) and co-founder and head of research at Fundstrat, modified his Ethereum (ETH) price outlook during an appearance at Token 2049 in Singapore. Lee stated that Ethereum should top $5,000 by year-end, linking his prediction to Bitcoin (BTC). 

This updated $5,000 target represents half of the $10,000 year-end figure Lee had previously characterized as a conservative target more than a week prior, when he also suggested the token would trade much higher within 12 months. Additionally, his longer-term cycle target was adjusted to $25,000 or $50,000 per token, down from an illustrative $62,000 baseline cited nine days earlier.

Despite the reduction in his numerical projections, Lee maintained an optimistic stance on overall digital asset valuations. He asserted that investors are not bullish enough and projected that market participants should expect some monster moves in crypto prices. Following his statements, Ethereum held above $2,400 on Saturday.
