Amazon has announced a plan to have outside investors finance about $8 billion of Nvidia Grace Blackwell chips, after which the company would lease the hardware back. The structure would make part of Amazon’s AI buildout more asset‑light at a time when its capital spending is expected to reach $220 billion this year.

## Impact on Earnings and Cash Flow
The proposal comes as Amazon’s second‑quarter 2026 results included $53.4 billion in non‑operating pretax income, largely from its Anthropic investment, making reported earnings a less reliable gauge of returns from AI infrastructure.

## Investor Confidence in Chip Value
Outside investors would need confidence that the Nvidia chips retain economic value beyond their first technological cycle. The proposal also introduces questions about residual chip value once the lease ends.

## What Readers Should Watch
Amazon’s CEO Andy Jassy has said the company still lacks enough capacity to satisfy demand even after increasing planned capital spending. The financing move could signal a shift in how Amazon balances heavy AI investment with cash‑flow considerations. Investors should monitor how the lease terms and residual values are structured and whether the deal materially changes Amazon’s capital allocation or earnings volatility.
