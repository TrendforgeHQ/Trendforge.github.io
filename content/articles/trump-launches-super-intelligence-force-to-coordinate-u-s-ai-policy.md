## Development of the Super Intelligence Force
In a recent executive order, former President Donald Trump announced the creation of the Super Intelligence Force (SIF). Jay Clayton, a former tech executive, will chair the body, effectively becoming the new AI/SI czar. The leadership roster also includes Federal Trade Commission Chair Andrew Ferguson, Undersecretary of War for Research and Engineering Emil Michael, and Office of Personnel Management Director Scott Kupor as vice chairs. 
The initiative follows Trump’s earlier promise in September to form an AI Force and appoint a czar, despite his dismissal of AI‑safety debates as a hoax. The administration has rebranded artificial intelligence as “super intelligence,” a terminology change Trump introduced at the United Nations General Assembly, arguing that the word “artificial” makes the technology sound fake. 

## Scope and Outreach
The SIF’s mandate is broad. Trump said the force would coordinate the federal government’s effort to ensure that America continues to lead the world in super intelligence, a technology many say surpasses the Industrial Revolution and the Internet. The force will engage with consumers, public‑interest groups, religious organizations, critical‑infrastructure providers, and super‑intelligence companies. 
External advisers to the SIF will include venture capitalist David Sacks and former Secretary of State Condoleezza Rice, who previously held the AI czar role at the start of Trump’s second term.
