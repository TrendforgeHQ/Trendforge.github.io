## Incident Details
A security guard is wanted by Philadelphia police after shooting a man during a confrontation outside a club on the 1000 block of Spring Garden Street early Sunday. Investigators say the guard pulled out a gun and shot the man before running from the scene. Officers responded to the location at about 12:30 a.m. for a reported shooting. According to investigators, the man had just left a party on the block when he became involved in a dispute with the guard.

## Wider Context
The incident adds to a series of recent shootings involving security personnel at Philadelphia bars, clubs, and other event venues. In July 2025, a physical fight on an exterior balcony at 7 Elements bar and lounge in South Philadelphia escalated into a mass shooting that injured eight people, including two 43‑year‑old male security guards. In October 2025, a female security guard at a Center City IHOP on Walnut Street fatally shot a male customer following a late‑night dispute. In 2025, the city passed a measure, Chapter 9‑6800, mandating additional safety training for commercial security officers, but enforcement is postponed until training‑provider criteria are finalized.

## Current Police Action
Philadelphia police Inspector D.F. said, “There’s a lot of work to be done, and we believe there is footage in the area that may have captured some or all of the incident.” Anyone with information about the shooting is asked to contact Philadelphia police.
