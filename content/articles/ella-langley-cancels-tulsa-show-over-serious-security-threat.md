## Sudden Cancellation
Ella Langley cancelled her sold‑out performance in Tulsa, Oklahoma, on Friday night because of a “serious security threat” that could endanger fans, venue staff, and her team. She announced the decision on social media hours before doors opened at the BOK Center. The venue echoed her post, stating that the safety and well‑being of everyone involved was the absolute priority and that the show could not proceed. Both Langley and the BOK Center said ticket‑refund information would be shared soon.

## What’s Known and What Remains Uncertain
The threat was reported by Tulsa police, who said a call was made that threatened the lives of people attending the event. The police also apologized to fans who had arrived for the concert. While the police noted that no credible threat had been found, the artist and venue still chose to cancel the show. Fans and ticket holders can still find remaining tickets for the rest of Langley’s “Dandelion” tour, and the artist has expressed regret for the disruption, acknowledging the time, travel, money, and planning that go into attending a concert.

## Practical Takeaway
If you were planning to attend the Tulsa show, check the venue’s or artist’s website for refund updates and consider purchasing tickets for future tour dates. While the broader tour is expected to proceed without further disruptions, the situation highlights how even well‑planned events can be halted by unforeseen security risks.
