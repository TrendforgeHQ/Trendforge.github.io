## SEC’s Stance on On‑Chain Fundraising
On September 29, SEC Chairman Paul Atkins said the agency will keep writing rules for on‑chain fundraising after Congress failed to pass a crypto market‑structure statute this month. He explained that, even though the CLARITY Act was not enacted, the SEC is proceeding to provide clarity to the marketplace regarding how to raise money on chain. Atkins described a proposed rule already out and a second track covering how tokenized securities trade.

## Tokenized Securities Framework
Atkins noted that the SEC’s framework will resolve the problem by requiring the token to carry the security’s actual rights. This requirement is what separates the American model from the offshore one. The SEC will only sanction the second kind of rule, which covers how tokenized securities trade, according to Atkins.
